2. **ICAR founding date and HQ not taught.** '16 July 1929' and 'headquarters New Delhi' appear only on icar.org.in (outside profile). The chapter teaches ICAR's premier-institution status and its NASC Complex, New Delhi foundation-day venue from PIB only. GK-IGK-201 untaught.
3. **BSI headquarters city not taught.** BSI's Kolkata HQ appears in address blocks/mastheads (e.g. its e-newsletter) that could not be fetched as page text in this run (two fetch attempts failed upstream). Establishment (1890 / 13 Feb 1890) is taught. GK-IGK-205 and the hub item GK-IGK-216 marked untaught.

GK-IGK-203 untaught.
5. **SIDBI operations date.** The bank item's framing is matched via the Act (39 of 1989) and its commencement (7 March 1990); the often-quoted '2 April 1990' incorporation date (sidbi.in) was not used.
6. **Exim Bank operations start.** India Code gives enforcement from 1 January 1982 (s.26 from 1 March 1982); the bank item's 'commenced operations March 1982' is marked partial in the map.
7. **NHRC street-level detail** is limited to the Commission's own letterhead (Manav Adhikar Bhawan, C-Block, GPO Complex, INA, New Delhi - 110023); no separate 'headquarters' statement was fetched.
8. **Not covered by design (summary scope):** Tea Board, NSE, railway zone HQs, CIWTC, CRRI, UDGAM portal, SEBI's 1992 statutory year, the Governor-General of 1784, and all international-organisation HQs (World Bank, BIMSTEC, APPU, WTO, UNHRC, IUCN) — the last group belongs to the Organisations book.
