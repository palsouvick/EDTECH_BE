### Notes on the imported papers

- **Scanned PDFs.** Both papers are image scans with no text layer. Questions were transcribed
  page by page from rendered images, and every question was cross-checked against the key
  letter for its booklet series. Each key letter was also checked to be a sensible answer, which
  confirms that the series matches (B for WBCS 2022, A for Clerkship 2023 Session I).
- **Clerkship 2019 — two questions excluded because the official key is factually wrong**
  (11 Nov 2026). Forenoon Q39 ("Who was not a famous sculptor?") — the key marks
  Ramkinkar Baij, who was a celebrated sculptor; the factually correct option is
  Tarasankar Bandyopadhyay (a novelist). Afternoon Q13 ("India's 'Look East' policy was
  launched by") — the key marks Narendra Modi; it was launched under P. V. Narasimha Rao.
  Both were left out rather than teach a wrong fact or break fidelity to the official
  key; including either (with the factually correct answer, flagged) is the project
  owner's decision.
- **Clerkship 2004–2009 — unofficial reproductions, owner-approved exception** (11 Nov 2026).
  The Commission never published these papers or any answer key for them (its previous-year
  page starts at 2019), so the standing official-only rule cannot be met for these years.
  With the project owner's approval they were imported anyway, labelled in the app as
  "Paper text from an unofficial copy" + "Answer cross-checked · no official key"
  (provenance ADAPTED_PYQ; the importer's unofficial-host allowance is restricted to
  wbpscupsc.com and englishsahayak.blogspot.com with no key cited — see
  `src/services/questionProvenance.js`). Question text comes from the wbpscupsc.com
  subject-wise reproductions; part of the 2009 Bengali text comes via
  englishsahayak.blogspot.com. Verification before import: every 2007/2009 item that an
  independent carrier (pyqarena.com) also lists was matched by question text and its
  answer compared (103 agreements); all remaining items (204) were independently
  re-solved (arithmetic) or fact-checked (GS/English), with era current-affairs items
  checked against contemporary reports — no recorded answer failed. Four disagreements
  were adjudicated on objective grounds and the reproduction's answer kept: 2007
  "Drain of Wealth" (Dadabhai Naoroji — the standard attribution; a second carrier's key
  says R. C. Dutt), 2009 "aware ___ his motive" (of), 2009 synonym of "Wrap" (cover),
  2009 "portable" (movable; the other carrier's answer belongs to its "potable" twin).
  One item was dropped because two unofficial keys directly conflict and the fact is
  genuinely debatable: 2009 Session I, "India's oldest civilisation" (Harappan vs
  Mehrgarh). Caveats: these are partial collections, not complete papers (the 2007 file
  does not preserve the sitting split — the exam was held in two sittings — and
  `pyq.question_no` is collection order, not the printed booklet number); 2009 Session I
  merges two reproduction batches of the same sitting.
- **Question numbers** (`pyq.question_no`) follow the series used: WBCS Series B and Clerkship
  Series A. The Clerkship PDF contains all four series (A to D); Series A is pages 1-20.
- **WBCS 2022 cover.** The booklet cover is printed "2021", but the site lists it as "WBCS(PRELI)-2022".
  The key notice names the exam "W.B.C.S. (Exe.) etc. (Preliminary) Examination, 2022", dated 19.06.2022.
  The questions match the key exactly, so the paper is tagged as year 2022.
- **Bengali text.**
  - WBCS 2022 Q26-200 and Clerkship Q31-100 are printed bilingually, and the Bengali in the
    files is the official Bengali text from the paper.
  - The English-language sections are printed in English only: WBCS Q1-25 and Clerkship Q1-30.
    For these, the Bengali instruction line is **our translation**, and the English sentences and
    options are left in English, because they are English-language test items.
  - WBCS Q20 is an English-only GS question; its Bengali stem and options are **our translation**.
  - Clerkship Q19 is an English-only GS question; its Bengali stem and options are **our translation**.
- **Printed quirks kept as printed:**
  - WBCS Q68 prints "Loringa" (meaning Coringa); the explanation says so.
  - WBCS Q121 has the Bengali "সাবেক".
  - WBCS Q136 prints "Bisthi" (Vishti).
  - WBCS Q98 uses the old National Highway numbering; the explanation notes this.
  - For underlined words (WBCS Q13; Clerkship Q10, 14, 16, 18, 21, 23, 27), the underlined
    part is given in brackets after the sentence.
- **Explanations** (English and Bengali) are ours, written to agree with the official key.
- **Low-confidence explanation:** WBCS Q172 (Howrah's child sex ratio, Census 2011). The key
  answer is used, but we could not confirm the exact Census figures, so the explanation gives none.

### Notes on Miscellaneous 2023 (Series A) — imported 16 Nov 2026

- **Scanned PDF.** The paper is an image scan with no text layer (PaperStream
  Capture). Questions were transcribed page by page from rendered images (200 dpi),
  English from the left column and Bengali from the right column, exactly as printed.
  The final key (Notice 651-PSC/CON(Q)) is a text PDF; its Series A column was
  machine-parsed from the PDF (bbox coordinates) and every one of the 97 imported
  answers was checked against it. Spot checks against known facts also agree
  (Q1 V. Gordon Childe, Q4 Ashoka, Q100 Vikram Sarabhai).
- **Q35 (clock angle at 9:15).** The exact angle is 187.5°, which is not among the
  options (100°, 115°, 360°, 180°); the key's 180° is the closest option, and the
  paper instructs candidates to pick the "correct or the best" answer. Kept, with an
  explanation that states the exact value and that 180° is the closest option the key
  marks. This is not a wrong-key case (contrast Clerkship 2019 below).
- **Printed as printed.** Q31 English is ungrammatical in the paper ("After going
  enlightenment…"); Q43 prints "Noble Prize" in English (Bengali correctly says
  নোবেল); Q48 option D carries a Bengali gloss "(দশমিক)" the English lacks; Q76
  ends mid-sentence in both languages ("is a famous Indian" / "বিখ্যাত ভারতীয়") with
  the options supplying the missing word. All transcribed verbatim, not fixed.
- **Q88 stem collision.** "Name the first newspaper in India." is word-for-word the
  stem of WBCS Prelims 2023 Q27. Both keys point to Bengal Gazette (Misc option A,
  WBCS option C), so the collision is benign — but the importer's stem identity means
  the bank keeps one document under the later file's (WBCS 2023) ref, and Misc shows
  96 questions in the bank rather than 97. Nothing was changed to work around it.
- **Assistant Engineer (Electrical) 2020 — NOT imported; the key does not answer the

### Notes on IDO 2019 (Series A)

- This is the only IDO paper the Commission lists on its Previous Year Question Paper
  page (checked 9 Nov 2026). It is the **Preliminary Screening Test** under the older
  Advt. 10/2019 scheme — one mixed 100-question paper, English only — **not** the
  seven-section scheme of the current Advt. 15/2024. The booklet's own instruction
  prescribes negative marking of ⅓ mark for every three wrong answers; the current
  advertisement prescribes −0.25 per wrong answer. The app presents the paper as a
  real previous-year paper and states both facts wherever the difference matters.
- The Bengali text is **our translation** throughout (the printed paper is
  English-only); the English stems and options are transcribed as printed.
- Answers follow the Commission's key (Notice No. 840-PSC/Con(Q), 27 Dec 2019).
  The key was first read from a mirror of the notice PDF; the same notice was
  then located on psc.wb.gov.in itself (What's New archive, Sl. No. 2462), its
  Series A column machine-parsed from the official PDF (100/100 values) and
  confirmed identical to the mirror parse, so the entries store the official
  `answer_key_url` and import as **OFFICIAL_VERIFIED**.
- Skipped (3): Q33 (time-sensitive — "largest trading partner of India"; the key's
  answer is pinned to its year and no longer true), Q38 (fill-in-the-blank whose
  printed options make the keyed answer nonsensical), Q90 (asks for an antonym of
  "Inclement" but the keyed answer is a synonym and no true antonym is offered).
- Five entries initially disagreed with the key and were corrected to it, with
  explanations rewritten to match: Q7 (Puranas), Q17 (Barrackpore), Q36 (mixed
  farming as keyed), Q53 (turmeric — root, as keyed), Q94 (PEOPLE code 125172,
  recomputed independently).
